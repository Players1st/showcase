Shinjuku is the busiest station in the world — and it's shared by four different operators, each with its own machines, fare rules, and IC card handling.

Switching the machine's display to English doesn't explain which machine to use, how fares differ between operators, or how to charge an IC card. TixGuide fills that gap — with step-by-step photo guides for the machine you're actually standing in front of.
